Before you go — Billy & Suz · Sat–Sun, Jul 11–12
  • Reserve the dinners. County rooms are tiny and summer weekends book up — lock Theia, Flame + Smith, Stella's, Quaich & Banter and Darlings in advance. Littlejohn Farm is pre-book only (Saturday farm dinners).
  • Two spots are closed Sunday — do them Saturday: Bloomfield Public House and Sprigwich. Everything else is open both days; the "Closed today" badges update live on each card.
  • Cluster by area. Towns are 15–25 min apart — Picton (hub), Wellington (west, on the lake), Bloomfield (between), wineries out in Hillier/Waupoos. Pick a town per outing; the maps pin every spot.
  • Allergens (Billy). County farm-to-table is mostly low-risk for your profile — proteins, produce, lake fish. The real watch-outs are flagged ⚠: tree nuts (Under the Vine's baklava), cashew & soy in vegan kitchens (Karlo, Bantam's tofu), Mexican mole (La Condesa), and bakery cross-contamination. Clams aren't a staple anywhere here — mussels, oysters and octopus are all clear for you.
  • 🌙 Nightlife is new. Twenty-one pubs, breweries, hotel bars, billiards and stages — jump to it. Three of them are closed on Sunday; the badges say which.
  • Make it special. Beyond dinners, the County does date nights well — the Mustang Drive-In, lavender fields in July bloom, Sandbanks beach & sunset, dancing at the Hayloft, cocktails at Bar Duchess. See Make It Special below. (Sandbanks day-use needs an advance reservation — book ahead.)
  • Antiquing — for you both. Your shared thing, and the County's one of Ontario's best hunting grounds; there's an Antiquing & Vintage section below (antique shops, vintage clothing, real thrift). Two are Saturday-only — Second Time Around and County RUST.
  • Everything is tappable → opens in Google Maps, with Directions and Call on each card.

Nightlife

21 rooms · pubs, breweries, hotel bars, billiards, stages
Read this before Saturday
  • Three of these are closed on your Sunday. Hartley's Tavern and Bloomfield Public House shut Sunday and Monday. The Lark opens Wednesday, Friday and Saturday only. If you want them, they are Saturday night — and the "Closed today" badges below update live.
  • Two are not in the County. The Lark (199 Front St) and Mr Zed's Billiard Room (322 Front St) are both in Belleville, roughly 35–40 minutes north of Picton across the bridge. They are worth knowing about; they are not a walk from dinner.
  • ⚠ Allergens, Billy — bars are different from kitchens. Two new risks the food guide doesn't cover. Cocktails: amaretto and orgeat are almond, Frangelico is hazelnut — any nutty, tiki, or amaretto-sour style drink is a hard no without asking. Parsons runs a Korean bowl; gochujang and soy sauce are soy protein, not lecithin. Bocado is Spanish tapas — romesco and picada are almond-and-hazelnut based, and Marcona almonds sit on most tables. La Condesa's mole is the peanut risk already flagged above.
  • What is reliably safe: beer, cider, wine, and straight spirits. The breweries and County Cider pour drinks with no nut or soy pathway at all. That's most of this list.
  • Eight of these already appear above as food cards — marked ↑. They're repeated here because the bar is a different proposition from the kitchen.

Make It Special — You & Suz

the County isn't just dinners — here's a perfect weekend for two
A County Datedrive-in · lavender · beach · live music · cocktails
Drive-In
Mustang Drive-In ★
The County date night — a lovingly restored drive-in in Bloomfield, double features under the stars, crisp FM sound, hot dogs & popcorn, dog-friendly. Open Fri–Sun, gates ~8pm, feature at dusk. Buy tickets online and check the weekend's lineup at mustangdrivein.com. Grab a Slickers cone first.
Lavender
PEC Lavender Farm
Purple fields in peak bloom in early–mid July — the most photogenic stop in the County (one for your camera) and a lovely slow morning with Suz. Hillier, open daily 10–5; free to wander, shop full of lavender goods. ⚑ Bloom timing shifts year to year — call ahead to confirm the fields are flowering before you drive out.
Beach
Sandbanks — Dunes & Outlet beaches
World-class freshwater dunes and soft-sand beach ~20 min south of Picton — a perfect summer afternoon and a stunning sunset. ⚑ Critical: summer day-use requires an advance vehicle reservation (reservations.ontarioparks.ca) — book before you go or you may be turned away. Pair with Sand and Pearl oysters just east.
Live Music
The Hayloft Dancehall
A restored barn dancehall in Cherry Valley — live bands, DJ nights, themed shows and a genuinely fun crowd; the County's best night out for dancing. Event-based — check the calendar for what's on your weekend and grab tickets ahead. (Eat first; the snack bar is basic.)
Cocktails
Bar Duchess
A moody, candlelit cocktail bar on Wellington Main — velvet couches, vintage portraits, expert cocktails (and oysters); the County's most romantic nightcap, perfect before or after dinner. Confirm current hours.
The Sweet Stopa County institution · two shops · open daily
Ice Cream
Slickers County Ice Cream
The County's cult small-batch ice cream — Campfire, Butter Tart, Apple Pie. Two shops: Picton (Main St, to 9:30pm) & Bloomfield (Main St, to 7:30pm), both open daily. ⚠ Heads-up for you: flavours include Maple Walnut, Mixed Nuts & Chocolate–Peanut Butter, all scooped in a tiny shop — a real cross-contamination risk. Ask how they handle nuts before ordering.

Antiquing & Vintage

your shared love — the County is one of Ontario's great antiquing & vintage-hunting grounds
🗺️ The Saturday Routeall 9, one loop, no backtracking · tap a stop to find it on the map
Why Saturday. Two of the nine are shut on Sunday — County RUST (Fri & Sat only) and Second Time Around. All nine are open Saturday.
Why this order. The two hardest closing times sit at opposite ends of the county: Second Time Around locks up at 3:00 PM in Picton, County RUST at 4:00 PM in Wellington. Sweeping east → west clears the 3 PM door before 11 AM and reaches the 4 PM door with ~40 minutes spare. Mary Street opens at 11:00 and lands third. The three Picton shops are a 5-minute walk apart — park once.
